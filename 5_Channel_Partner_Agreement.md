## **1. DEFINITIONS**

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **"Lead"** | Prospective customer contact (Name, Phone, Address, Roof Type, Bill Amount) submitted via Partner Portal/WhatsApp |
| **"Qualified Lead"** | Lead meeting: Homeowner, Roof Ownership, ₹1,500+ monthly bill, 300+ sq ft shadow-free area |
| **"Commissioned Installation"** | System fully installed, commissioned, net metering applied, handover completed |
| **"Partner Portal"** | WhatsApp Business API / Web dashboard for lead submission & tracking |
| **"Commission"** | Payment earned per qualified lead / commissioned installation per Schedule A |

## **2. PARTNER OBLIGATIONS**

### **2.1 Lead Generation**
- Submit minimum **5 qualified leads/month** via Partner Portal
- Provide accurate customer data: Name, Phone, Village/City, Roof Type, Monthly Bill, Landmark
- Pre-qualify: Confirm homeowner status, roof ownership, interest level (1-5 scale)
- Accompany BDA on first site visit if requested (paid ₹500/visit)

### **2.2 Brand Representation**
- Display co-branded board: "Authorized Channel Partner - Shubham Solar" at shop/office
- Use only approved marketing materials (provided by Company)
- Not make claims beyond approved spec: subsidies, savings, timelines
- Wear branded jacket/cap during customer interactions

### **2.3 Customer Service**
- Be first point of contact for assigned customers
- Assist in document collection (Aadhaar, Bill, Property docs)
- Coordinate site access for survey/installation teams
- Escalate complaints to Company within 4 hours

### **2.4 Compliance**
- Maintain valid electrical/plumbing license
- Follow safety protocols during any site assistance
- Not engage in parallel agreements with competing solar companies
- Maintain customer data confidentiality (DPDP Act compliant)

---

## **3. COMPANY OBLIGATIONS**

### **3.1 Support & Enablement**
- Provide: Co-branded visiting cards, brochures, banner, safety gear
- Conduct: Monthly 2-hour training (Technical + Sales + Subsidy Process)
- Assign: Dedicated Partner Success Manager (WhatsApp/Call support)
- Share: Real-time lead status via Partner Portal

### **3.2 Technical & Operational**
- Handle all: Design, Engineering, Procurement, Installation, Commissioning
- Manage: Subsidy filing (PM Surya Ghar, PM-KUSUM), Net Metering, DISCOM liaison
- Provide: 25-year performance warranty, 5-year workmanship warranty
- Offer: AMC at Partner-discounted rate (₹200/kW/yr vs ₹300/kW/yr)

### **3.3 Payment & Reporting**
- Pay commissions per Schedule A within 7 days of trigger event
- Provide monthly commission statement (1st of each month)
- Year-end TDS certificate (Form 16A) by May 31
- Annual Partner Meet + Awards (Top 3 per district)

## **5. LEAD VALIDATION PROCESS**

```
Partner Submits Lead (Portal/WhatsApp)
         ↓
Company Validates (24 hrs): Homeowner? Roof? Bill>₹1500? Area>300sqft?
         ↓
├── APPROVED → "Qualified Lead" → ₹3,000/₹5,000 paid
└── REJECTED → Reason shared (Not homeowner / Low bill / Rental / No roof access)
         ↓
BDA Contacts Customer (48 hrs) → Site Survey Booked
         ↓
Survey Done → ₹1,000/₹2,000 paid to Partner
         ↓
Proposal → Negotiation → Contract Signed → ₹2,000/₹5,000 paid
         ↓
Installation → Commissioning → Handover → ₹10,000/₹15,000/₹25,000 paid
```

## **8. TERM & TERMINATION**

### **8.1 Term**
- Initial: 12 months from Effective Date
- Auto-renewal: 12 months unless 30-day written notice by either party

### **8.2 Termination for Cause (Immediate)**
- Material breach (non-compliance, misrepresentation, parallel agreement)
- Loss of professional license
- Fraudulent lead submission
- Customer complaints (3+ verified in 6 months)

### **8.3 Termination for Convenience**
- Either party: 30-day written notice
- Company may terminate territory with 60-day notice for business restructuring

### **8.4 Effect of Termination**
- Accrued commissions paid per Schedule A
- Partner returns all branded materials
- Non-solicitation: 12 months, assigned territory (Company customers)
- Confidentiality: Perpetual

## **9. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION**

### **9.1 Confidential Information**
- Customer data (PII, bills, property docs)
- Pricing, margins, commission structure
- Technical designs, supplier details
- Business strategies, expansion plans

### **9.2 Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)**
- Partner = Data Processor, Company = Data Fiduciary
- Collect only: Name, Phone, Address, Bill, Roof photos
- Store: Max 30 days on personal device, then delete
- Share: Only via Partner Portal (encrypted)
- Breach notification: Within 24 hours to Company

## **10. INDEMNIFICATION & LIABILITY**

### **10.1 Partner Indemnifies Company Against:**
- Claims from Partner's negligence/misconduct
- Tax liabilities (GST, TDS) on Partner's income
- Customer complaints due to Partner's misrepresentation
- IP infringement from unauthorized material use

### **10.2 Company Indemnifies Partner Against:**
- Third-party IP claims on Company-provided materials
- Product liability (panels, inverters, structure)
- Installation defects (workmanship warranty)

### **10.3 Limitation of Liability**
- Company max liability: Total commissions paid in last 12 months
- No consequential damages (lost profits, reputation)
- Force majeure: Neither party liable (pandemic, natural disaster, policy change)
