## 5. RIPS-2024 REGISTRATION (Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme)

### Eligibility Criteria
- [ ] New Pvt Ltd/LLP in Rajasthan (not branch)
- [ ] Minimum investment: ₹1 Crore (Plant & Machinery) OR 25 employees
- [ ] Manufacturing/Services in notified sectors (Solar EPC qualifies under "Renewable Energy")
- [ ] Commercial production/operations within 3 years

### Benefits for Solar EPC
| Incentive | Quantum | Duration |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Investment Subsidy** | 30% of eligible investment (Plant & Machinery) | Max ₹50 Lakh |
| **SGST Reimbursement** | 75% of net SGST paid | 7 years |
| **Employment Subsidy** | ₹15,000/employee/year (min 25 employees) | 7 years |
| **Stamp Duty Exemption** | 100% on loan/lease/land docs | One-time |
| **Land Conversion Charges** | 100% exemption | One-time |
| **Electricity Duty Exemption** | 100% for captive consumption | 7 years |

### Application Process
1. **Pre-registration**: Submit DPR (Detailed Project Report) on RIPS portal
2. **Acknowledgment**: Receive RIPS ID within 15 days
3. **Commercial Operations**: Start operations within 3 years
4. **Claim Filing**: Quarterly/Annual claims via portal

### Required Documents for RIPS
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] MOA/AOA (showing solar/EPC in object clause)
- [ ] Board Resolution authorizing RIPS application
- [ ] Detailed Project Report (DPR) - 50+ pages
- [ ] Land documents (ownership/lease ≥ 10 years)
- [ ] Building plan approval
- [ ] Environmental clearance (if applicable)
- [ ] Power sanction letter
- [ ] Machinery purchase invoices (post-installation)
- [ ] Chartered Engineer certificate (capital expenditure)
- [ ] CA certificate (expenditure verification)
- [ ] Employment records (PF/ESIC challans)

### DPR Outline (Critical for Approval)
```
1. Executive Summary
2. Promoter Background & Experience
3. Project Description (Solar EPC + O&M)
4. Market Analysis (Rajasthan solar potential)
5. Technical Details (Installation capacity, equipment)
6. Financial Projections (3 years P&L, Cash Flow, Balance Sheet)
7. Investment Plan (Phase-wise, Capex breakup)
8. Employment Generation Plan
9. Implementation Timeline (Gantt)
10. Risk Analysis & Mitigation
11. Social & Environmental Impact
```

### Timeline & Cost
| Step | Timeline | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| DPR Preparation | 7-10 days | ₹25,000-50,000 (CA/Consultant) |
| Portal Registration | 1 day | Free |
| Application Filing | 1 day | Free |
| Acknowledgment | 15 days | Free |
| **Total** | **~3 weeks** | **~₹30,000-50,000** |
